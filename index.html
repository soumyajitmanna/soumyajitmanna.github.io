About

Soumyajit Manna is a PhD student at the University of Alabama, where he investigates hydrological drought and low flow dynamics across the contiguous United States. His academic journey spans IIT Bhubaneswar, Texas A&M University, and now Alabama — working to understand and uncover how low flow and drought processes emerge from catchment-scale hydrology and manifest at large scales, with direct implications for water resources.
